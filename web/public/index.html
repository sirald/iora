A scaled order splits one amount into several limit orders across a price range.

One transaction per fill

Every fill is one transaction. The vault leaves the lending pool, swaps and checks your limit together, and if the swap returns less than your limit, nothing moves.

Interest on a waiting order

An order book pays nothing while your order waits. In a vault the same funds earn the lending pool's rate, which moves with borrowing.

  • A buy order earns on the USDC that waits.
  • A sell order earns on the coin that waits.
  • The interest stays in your vault with the funds.

Questions

The docs answer each of these in full.

Read the docs

Can I lose money on Iora?

Yes. Every vault supplies to one lending pool, and bad debt there would reduce each deposit in it. A contract bug could cause a loss too.

Is Iora audited?

Not yet. It runs on testnet with test funds, and mainnet waits for an independent audit and its published report.

Does Iora hold my funds?

No. They sit in your own vault contract and, through it, in the pool. Only your wallet can withdraw them.

Where does the interest come from?

From borrowers on Blend, the pool your vault lends to. The rate moves with how much of it is borrowed. An asset the pool does not list waits without earning.

What if my price never comes?

The order keeps waiting until you cancel it. The funds earn the whole time, and you withdraw them with the interest.

    How borrowing works

    1. Supply first. What you supply is your collateral. Each asset counts for only part of its value (its collateral factor), so a 1,000 USD deposit with a 75% factor lets you borrow up to about 750 USD of effective debt.
    2. Borrow less than your limit. Debt grows with interest and prices move. The further your health factor stays above 1, the safer you are.
    3. Watch your health factor. If it drops below 1, liquidators can buy part of your collateral at a discount to repay your debt. You keep the rest, but you lose value.
    4. Repay any time. Repaying more than you owe is fine; Blend returns the extra in the same transaction.

    Rates are estimates from Blend's rate model and change all the time. Prices come from the pool's oracle. Blend's borrowing guide

        Before you continue

        Iora is a non-custodial interface to smart contracts on Stellar. Please read this once.

        • Testnet only. Everything here uses free test assets with no real value. Never send real funds to a testnet address.
        • Not audited yet. The contracts are tested, but no independent security audit has been done.
        • Protocol risk. Funds sit in a Blend lending pool shared by all vaults. Bad debt or low liquidity there can affect them.
        • You stay in control. Every action needs your signature in your wallet. We will never ask for your recovery phrase.
